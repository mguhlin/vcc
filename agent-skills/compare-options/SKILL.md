# Compare Options

Adapted by Miguel Guhlin for VCC from [Compare options](https://github.com/mguhlin/creations/blob/main/architect/skills/packages/compare-options-skill/SKILL.md) by Miguel Guhlin (CC0-1.0). This self-contained adaptation omits the upstream practice-file dependency.

## Purpose

Help someone make an informed choice while keeping facts, preferences, estimates, and unknowns distinct.

## Workflow

1. Use the options and priorities already provided. Separate must-have constraints from preferences. Ask a focused question only if a missing requirement could change the choice; otherwise state a provisional assumption.
2. Build a compact comparison table using criteria that matter to this person. Include keeping the current approach or trying a small experiment where realistic.
3. Verify current costs, availability, or specifications against primary sources when they affect the decision and browsing is available. Otherwise mark those cells unverified.
4. Eliminate an option only if it fails a stated constraint. Explain which priorities favor each remaining option.
5. Use numerical weights only when supplied or agreed to by the user. Do not invent objective-looking scores.
6. Identify the unknown most likely to change the choice and a low-cost way to resolve it.
7. Offer a conditional recommendation, one practical next action, and the condition that would change the recommendation. Leave the final decision with the user.

## Output

- Decision and stated constraints
- Options-by-criteria table, with evidence and unknowns
- Tradeoffs tied to the user's priorities
- Conditional recommendation
- Small test or next action

## Safeguards

Do not invent prices, specifications, scores, or supporting sources. Do not hide assumptions. For medical, legal, or financial matters, distinguish information gathering from qualified professional judgment. Keep private information out of external searches unless authorized.

## Example Requests

"Compare these three classroom project ideas against our time and device constraints."

"Help us choose an option and identify a small trial before committing."
