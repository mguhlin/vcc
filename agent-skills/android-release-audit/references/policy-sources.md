# Primary policy references

Read the relevant current page when applying a policy. Record the applicable rule and evidence in the review; preserve substantive policy deadlines only when they affect that decision. These links do not establish universal applicability or approval.

- [Play Protect developer warning guidance](https://developers.google.com/android/play-protect/warning-dev-guidance): distinguish warning categories and the official appeal process.
- [Play Protect warning strings](https://developers.google.com/android/play-protect/warning-strings): exact diagnostic text matters.
- [Google Play Protect user guidance](https://support.google.com/android/answer/2812853): scanning and user-facing protection.
- [Mobile Unwanted Software principles](https://developers.google.com/android/play-protect/mobile-unwanted-software): transparency, consent, behavior and interference.
- [REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12085295): restricted core-purpose uses and declarations; a signed user-confirmed self-updater is not a Play exception.
- [Device and Network Abuse](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888379): Play self-update and executable-code rules, interpreter caveats and device behavior.
- [Target API requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11926878): recheck the actual product/channel/API floor; do not freeze it in the skill.
- [Data Safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469): app and SDK data declarations; verify what leaves the device.
- [Account-deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111): determine whether app-account creation triggers them.
- [Android signing](https://developer.android.com/studio/publish/app-signing): distinguish package signature, Play App Signing, upload key, publisher verification and updates.
- [Android permissions](https://developer.android.com/privacy-and-security/permissions): scoped authority and denial/revocation.
- [Google Play Developer Policy Center](https://play.google.com/about/developer-content-policy/): billing, ads, content, audience, Families and AI rules when those features exist.
- [Google Play SDK Index](https://play.google.com/sdks): relevant indexed SDK evidence, not blanket certification of every dependency.
- [OAuth verification](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/production-readiness/sensitive-scope-verification): direct API scope authorization; separate from a selected shared file.
- [Workspace Marketplace review](https://developers.google.com/workspace/marketplace/about-app-review): Workspace integrations/listings, distinct from APK review and Play Protect classification.

Do not reuse third-party instructions to bypass warnings or silently submit/scan an owner's APK on an external service. References provide policy evidence; account-specific status requires owner/Console evidence.
