# About VCC

The **Vibe-Coding Center (VCC)** is a growing collection of guidance, reusable instructions, and practical tools for educators creating websites with AI. Vibe coding means describing what you want to an AI coding assistant and refining what it builds. VCC helps you keep educational purpose, design, accessibility, privacy, and security in that conversation.

VCC includes three core instruction guides and a companion [Vibe-Code Rescue guide](rescue.html) for reproducible setup, useful diagnostics, and verified repairs to existing projects.

## A center that can grow

VCC starts with the foundations of a useful educational website. New guides can address classroom testing, local-first tools, hosting, and maintenance as those resources are developed. Additions belong in the library, with a clear audience, downloadable files, source notes, and honest testing limits.

[Suggest a resource or report a problem](https://github.com/mguhlin/vcc/issues) · [Browse the source repository](https://github.com/mguhlin/vcc)

### Does this center collect student information?

This site has no account, upload form, analytics script, remote font, or application database. Search and reminders run in your browser without saving their contents. GitHub Pages receives normal hosting requests. External sites receive requests when you follow their links. Keep student information out of public issues and AI prompts.

### Does using these files make my site compliant?

The guides help you identify risks and gather evidence. Legal requirements, contracts, school authorization, and technical controls need separate review appropriate to your project. Use the official sources linked in the [education guide](education.html).

### Can I use VCC without a coding assistant?

Yes. The introductions, planning templates, and review questions also work as a checklist for conversations with a developer or school technology team.
