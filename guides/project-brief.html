Project brief

This is your project's plan: what you are building, who it serves, its data boundaries, and the evidence of success. It is separate from WEB_DESIGN.md, which tells the assistant how to approach design.

Copy this file to your project root as PROJECT_BRIEF.md. Replace the prompts below with your decisions. Keep student information out of the brief. Put it alongside WEB_DESIGN.md, EDU_COMPLIANCE.md, and securevcc.md before using the starting prompt.

One useful task

What should an educator, learner, or family member accomplish? Describe one observable outcome. Example: a teacher creates a printable vocabulary activity without signing in.

AI invocation

Read WEB_DESIGN.md, EDU_COMPLIANCE.md, and securevcc.md. Apply them to this brief. Start by inspecting the project. Do not invent approvals, test results, or legal claims. Explain the proposed structure, build a small working version, and report evidence and limitations.
