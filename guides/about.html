About VCC

The Vibe-Coding Center (VCC) is a growing collection of guidance, reusable instructions, and practical tools for educators creating websites with AI. Vibe coding means describing what you want to an AI coding assistant and refining what it builds. VCC helps you keep educational purpose, design, accessibility, privacy, and security in that conversation.
